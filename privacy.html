privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers the 00 Cleared member site at www.advanced-amd.com, run by Josh Painter, San Antonio, Texas, USA. You can reach us by messaging us in the 00 Cleared Discord server.

2. What we collect

You sign in with Discord. When you approve sign-in, Discord shares the following with us, and nothing else (we never see your Discord password, messages or email address):

DataWhy we use it
Discord user ID, username and display nameTo identify your account and greet you
Profile picture and bannerTo show your profile in the portal
The date you joined our server and your roles in itTo confirm you're a member and decide which chapters you can open

We also record, linked to your Discord ID:

  • Your progress: which lessons, breakdowns and homework you've completed, and the date of each.
  • When you last signed in.

Your browser keeps a few things on your own device to make the site work: how far you've watched each video, the lesson you were last on, and announcements you've dismissed. These stay on your device and you can clear them by clearing site data in Chrome.

3. Cookies

We use two cookies, both needed for sign-in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • cleared_session keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. It can't be read by page scripts.
  • cleared_oauth_state protects the sign-in step and expires after 10 minutes.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and give you access that matches your Discord roles.
  • To track and show your progress, on your profile and to us, so we can support your learning.
  • To run, secure and improve the site.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services below that run the site.

5. Services that process data for us

  • Discord: sign-in and server roles.
  • Vercel: hosting the site, running sign-in, and storing lesson videos (Vercel Blob).
  • Upstash: the database that stores member records and progress.
  • Google Fonts: loads the site's fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.
  • Payments are handled outside this site by the checkout you buy from, under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your card details.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your member record and progress while you're a member and for 12 months after you leave, so your progress is still there if you come back. After that it's deleted. Ask us and we'll delete it sooner.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See or delete your data: message us in the 00 Cleared Discord server from the account you signed in with, and we'll send you a copy or delete it.
  • Remove our access: in Discord, go to User Settings → Authorized Apps and remove 00 Cleared. Signing out of the site also ends your session.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as correcting your data or objecting to how it's used. Contact us to use them.

8. Security

Sign-in sessions are signed and sent only over HTTPS, access checks happen on our server, and lesson videos for chapters you can't open are never sent to your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Discord account protected with a strong password and two-factor authentication.

9. Age

The site is for adults. It isn't intended for anyone under 18, and Discord itself requires users to be at least 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and post an announcement for significant changes.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests? Reach us by messaging us in the 00 Cleared Discord server.
